Brief History
Best Tutorial Center was founded in November 2016 by Adisa A. Johnson which was regarded as Student Progressive Group(SPG) with about 10 - 15 members having her meeting every Saturday 9 am at Torokay Shed, Faculty of Agriculture which was presided by the founder.
BTC has been helping countless number of people academically, Spiritually, financially and career. It was registered that BTC paid a student tuition fee by partnering with an organisation Great People's Foundation(GPF) in 2019.
The name of the organisation was later changed fron Student Progressive Group to Best-Man-Tutorial not until 2019 which was now changed to Best Tutorial Center having her official President under the leadership of Morakinyo.S. Uchenna on May 13, 2023. The second induction was conducted on 29th January, 2024 under the leadership of Alaka Emmanuel Iyanuoluwa.
Best Tutorial Center has been known for unusual brotherly affections among her members. The Matron of BTC id DR.MRS Gegeleso from the department of transport management.
